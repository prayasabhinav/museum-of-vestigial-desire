# Post-events in multiverses

In this time, we know that there are also other times. This is not the only concurrent moment, there are various other moments operating simultaneously. Any event that takes place will not take place in this time alone, it will either happen simultaneously in all the times accessible. This concurrence takes away from the event's thunder. The event has a thunder only when it is understood to be a singular occurrence. The moment the event is fragmented, it does not remain singularly important anymore. 

To not deal with events which feel that they are singular is a blessing in disguise. To deal with adamant entities is a pain. It is draining and it exhausts the breath. 

Events that agree to accept themselves as only parallels of an infinite array are pleasant to deal with. 

But such events are difficult to come across. We come across only that which we can deal with. We cannot deal with events which accept their own vulnerability. We cannot accept experiences that do not pitch themselves strongly. 

 We do not have the skills of dealing with daft and empty-hearted events. We feel confused. We are unable to do anything to respond. 

Because we feel challenged, we complain. We crib and refuse to deal with things the way they are. Our complaining and the sound of our general hue and cry distracts the crowd only for an instance. Then the crowd recedes back into a shroud again. 

After the commotion is over, we start dealing with the problem again. We only want to deal with daft events because we want to be able to deal with everything. We want to have dexterity. We do not want to be stuck in an ecosystem where we cannot interact with every unit of experience. 

Over time we learn to interact with events which accept their true nature. We don't require any external intervention for doing so, just observing and making connections is enough. Events that accept their own true nature are not made to court a big audience. They are not meant to operate as a mechanisms to connect with the populace. If any such desire to amplify their value and exposure exists, the event in the multiverse asks us to concoct a strategy keeping in mind it's actual status. 

For this event, which considers and understand itself to be only a parallel and nothing else, it is difficult to assume and imagine a primacy. It dismisses itself as we do not yet have the ability to access different time-zones. We live in this time, but we believe that there are so many other time zones which cast their shadow in many ways, we try to trace the shadow of that which is certainly there but has not been accessed yet. We remain in the dark, we are not able to trace the source of the darkness. 

For this event, experience is fractured. The other parts of this fractured experience are not available but we have to live in their shadow.
