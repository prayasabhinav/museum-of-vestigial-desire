# Bairang

A letter sent without enough postage. Whoever receives it pays, or it goes back.

Bairang is a band of individual practitioners, each doing their own thing, who show and publish together in episodes. They research, write, make sound, make things, teach or write code. I do not invite artists, and I do not invite art spaces. Bairang is not a way of getting artists to do conceptual work. I curate it, and I am also one of the people in it. It continues what I called [self-validation](https://museumofvestigialdesire.net/functions/laborataries/the-surface-of-knowledge/selfvalidation) here in 2013.

Each invitation carries a provocation and asks for one work in response to it. There is no open call. You can propose yourself as somebody I should invite, and a space that is not an art space, such as a library, a school or can say what it can offer an episode. I write to spaces and ask what they could offer. I invite them to apply or pitch.
