# Mystery

We are already going to keep this word. Our sieve will not spit it out. We know and we have declared our bias. Declaring our bias is a part of our strategy to keep you, our confidante, in congruence with where we believe we are headed. This book is not a mystery, our message is clearly out. The future is not distant. We are living in a universe with multiple possible outcomes and it is possible that we are living the outcome of only one of them. and it is possible that our past is your future and your future is someone else’s present. We believe that the future might be a witness to our present because when we navigate through all the new innovations that humans have come upon, they go through the same basic problems. They all feel lonely, they all feel lost and confused and they all despair.
