# Me

After we were forced to step into ourselves, we discovered who we are. In the continuum of time, we are currently situated at a very specific point. Although there is a kind of scale involved, we cannot determine exactly where we are on the continuum of time. We do not know that when our speech oversteps its duration of utterance, we form a bridge to the future. Just by elongating the duration of the delivery os sound. Or singing as some people call it. Just by performing this small step, we tangentially connect to the future. 

Song is with music or with harmonious sound of a kind. We avoid music, w just groan with rough sound because music is a set pattern. It does not allow for a lot of flexibility in the successive positioning of notes and scales. It forces an aesthetic onto us and we do not like that.
