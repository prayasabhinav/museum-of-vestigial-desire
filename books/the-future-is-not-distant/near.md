# Near

We are already going to keep this word. Our sieve will not spit it out. We know and we have declared our bias. Declaring our bias is a part of our strategy to keep you, our confidante, in congruence with where we believe we are headed. This book is not a mystery, our message is clearly out. The future is not distant. We are living in a universe with multiple possible outcomes and it is possible that we are living the outcome of only one of them. and it is possible that our past is your future and your future is someone else’s present. We believe that the future might be a witness to our present because when we navigate through all the new innovations that humans have come upon, they go through the same basic problems. They all fee lonely, they all feel lost and confused and they all despair. 

Somewhere the sun is rising and somewhere the sun has set. Time is out of sync because we are not living on a flat planet. But things take time to get around. And because things take time, everything gets equalised and levelled. Stories that we tell are not bumpy because these stories are already many steps removed from our lives. We do not believe in realism because realism does not exist. Everything is distorted at a certain scale and a certain magnification. The microscopic plane of materiality is wavering in its scope. But what manages to get across is not the fact but the fabricated story only. We can never reach facts, only stories. 

We are near a certain set of stories because we have embraced the stories as if they were our mythology. We have made these stories the set of stories which contain the only filtered and reduced truth that is known about us.
